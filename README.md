# Daniel Jimmie

I’m an executive operator designing AI-enabled business systems across revenue, client relationships, delivery, talent, operations, and organizational decision-making.

For more than 15 years, I helped build and scale custom software, digital product, and UX consulting businesses—growing revenue, expanding strategic accounts, assembling multidisciplinary teams, leading delivery, and creating the operating capabilities required as the organizations matured.

I’m now applying that experience to a different question:

> How can AI help organizations connect fragmented information, interpret what matters, make better decisions, coordinate action, and learn from the results?

## What I’m building

My current work includes:

* **ClarusOps** — an evolving growth and operations practice focused on turning scattered commercial and operational signals into clearer decisions, ownership, and execution.
* **Mammoth Home Buyers + Mhoment Realty** — a shared real-estate opportunity environment supporting two distinct commercial motions within one operating system.
* **InMotion Commercial + Gaming** — a growth-intelligence demonstration connecting market signals, organizational conditions, opportunities, strategy, action, and outcomes across two business lines.
* **Opportunity Intelligence Systems** — operating frameworks that transform large, ambiguous information environments into structured attention, decisions, and action.

## How I work

My work combines:

* Business and operating-model design
* Workflow and decision architecture
* Data structures and systems of record
* Human-in-the-loop AI design
* Prompt and context engineering
* Operating controls and exception handling
* Implementation planning and configuration
* Testing, rehearsal, documentation, and iteration

This is human-directed, AI-assisted work. I define the operating problems, supply the business context, challenge assumptions, make the decisions, test the systems against operating reality, and determine what becomes part of the working model.

## What this profile will document

This GitHub profile is a living record of the systems, frameworks, implementation artifacts, lessons, and decisions emerging from that work.

Some projects began before this GitHub account existed. Historical materials will be added with their original dates and provenance clearly identified rather than represented as historical GitHub activity.

The goal is not to present myself as a traditional software engineer. It is to demonstrate how an experienced operator can use AI and modern technical tools to turn business judgment into systems people can actually operate.
